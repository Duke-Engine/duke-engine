What was changed
----------------

The originals are drawn white on an opaque black square. The square is gone: a
slot in this game's panel is carved stone, and a black tile laid over it is a
hole rather than an icon.

Each file here is that one shape at 128x128, white on transparent -- white so
that one file can serve all three states of a slot, which the panel tints as it
draws: torch for ready, cold grey while it reloads, nearly out while it is still
locked.

The drawings themselves are untouched.
